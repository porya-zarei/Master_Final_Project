# M3.3-B — state-aware conservative allocation, closed loop

> **ERRATA — 2026-11-06, from the M3.4 mathematical audit (`M34_RLS_uncertainty_audit.md`).** Three statements in the sections below are superseded and must not be quoted:
>
> 1. *"the estimates are good"* — over 24 seeds the effectiveness estimate is **biased high at h=0.5** (bias +0.106, MAE 0.106, max error 0.308, min h_hat 0.533): it never reaches the truth. The single-seed value that suggested otherwise was optimistic.
> 2. *P1 = PASS* — **vacuous**, not evidence: no wheel is ever certified, so "no false alarm" is unfalsifiable in this configuration.
> 3. The root cause of the closed gate is **not** a threshold that is merely too tight: `sigma_h` has units of (N·m)⁻¹ and the gate is dimensionally invalid (same physics reported as 11.55 in N·m and 0.0124 in mN·m).
>
> The tables and all per-episode numbers below are valid and unchanged.

## Arms

- `oracle` — allocation from ground-truth wheel health (privileged upper bound).
- `unity` — allocator believes all wheels healthy, never corrected (no-fault-knowledge lower bound).
- `estimated` — per-wheel joint RLS `(h, tau_f)` -> state-aware policy (the only deployable arm).

## Caveats that travel with these numbers

- The estimator reads the plant's wheel speed. There is **no tachometer** in `sensors.py`; this is an idealised readout. `--tachometer noisy` is the stated sensitivity case.

- Faults are static inside an episode except S7, so this does not exercise tracking of a time-varying health.

- Disturbance flags (aero / SRP / residual dipole) are **no-ops** (G9) and no plant uncertainty is present (G10). Nothing here claims robustness to either.

- The `DEGRADED` floor caps amplification at 2.0x, which by construction cannot route a *total* wheel failure's residual to the magnetorquers. S6 is the scenario that measures that cost.

- Episodes are independent IC draws because no policy is learned here; this is a classical-controller comparison, not a method-level RL claim.
